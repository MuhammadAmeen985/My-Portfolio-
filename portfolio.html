Who I Am

Engineer by degree,

I'm a final-year Mechanical Engineering student at PIET, Multan (CGPA 3.52/4.00), graduating in early 2025. My academic journey spans thermal systems, fluid mechanics, HVAC design, and applied mechanics — but I've refused to stop there.

Over the past 5+ years as a Mechanical System Supervisor at Fazal Group, I've maintained industrial-scale HVAC equipment — chillers, AHUs, cooling towers — and developed a deep intuition for how these systems fail. That experience pushed me to bridge the physical and digital worlds: building an AI-powered Thermal Event Detection System using Python and Raspberry Pi that brings real-time diagnostics to industrial HVAC setups.

"Every physical system has data waiting to be listened to — I just learned to listen."

My long-term goal is to found Smart Line Automation — a startup laser-focused on intelligent HVAC automation and smart manufacturing systems. I believe the future of engineering lies at the intersection of precision mechanics and artificial intelligence.

Founder: Muhammed Ameen

Smart Line Automation is more than a business plan — it's the culmination of 5+ years of witnessing inefficiency up close. Every compressor failure I've diagnosed, every energy waste I've measured — they all point to the same root problem: industrial HVAC systems are still mostly dumb.

The vision: build a full-stack intelligent automation platform that turns legacy HVAC infrastructure into predictive, self-optimizing systems. From edge-level sensor intelligence (Raspberry Pi nodes) to cloud-based dashboards and AI-driven maintenance scheduling — every layer reimagined.

Initial market focus: textile manufacturing facilities in Punjab, Pakistan — where HVAC failure costs are enormous and digital transformation is just beginning. Then: broader South Asian industrial markets.
